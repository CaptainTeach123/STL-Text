      3 Text & fonts

      Lines – each with its own font and size

        Press Enter in a line for a new line below it. Fonts: any .ttf / .otf / .woff file works – upload it or drop it on the page; it is applied to the current line.

        4 Raised or cut

        Raised text sits on the plate; cut-in text is engraved into it. The plate follows curved surfaces, so a banner wraps around a cane or a mug.

          Advanced

          5 Placement

          Click the model to put the text there, or drag the text. Drag empty space to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom.
